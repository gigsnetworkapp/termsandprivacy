Introduction & Scope

GigsNetwork ("we", "our", "us") operates a two-sided marketplace mobile application that connects Workers seeking short-term, gig, and event-based employment with Recruiters who post and manage such opportunities.

This Privacy Policy explains in full detail what personal data we collect when you use our Android application, why we collect it, how we use and share it, how long we retain it, and what rights you have over your data. Please read it carefully. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

Important Notice

GigsNetwork is a platform intermediary. We facilitate connections between Workers and Recruiters but are not a party to any employment relationship, wage agreement, or on-site conduct. Specific obligations regarding payments, safety, and conduct are detailed in Section 6.

Information We Collect

We only collect data that is necessary for the app to function as described. Below is a complete breakdown of every category of data we collect.

Account
Phone Number

Your mobile phone number is the only way to create and access a GigsNetwork account. It is used exclusively for OTP-based authentication. No email or password is used.

Account
Full Name

Your name is mandatory. It is displayed on your profile and shared with Recruiters or Workers you interact with through job enrollments.

Account
Gender

Your gender is mandatory. It is used solely to determine your eligibility for jobs that specify a gender requirement (e.g., a recruiter may post a job open only to female workers). It is not used for advertising or profiling.

Account
Role

Whether you operate as a Worker, Recruiter, or both. A single account can hold both roles and switch between them.

Account
Profile Picture

An optional photo you choose to upload. Displayed on your profile and visible to the other party in job enrollments.

Location
State & City

You manually select your state and city from a list. We do not access your device's GPS or real-time location at any time. This is used to surface relevant nearby jobs.

Recruiter Only
Organization Name

The name of the organization or business under which a Recruiter posts jobs. Visible to Workers browsing job listings.

Activity
Jobs & Enrollments

Jobs you post as a Recruiter, jobs you enroll in as a Worker, attendance records, and ratings you give or receive after job completion.

Communications
Chat Messages

Messages you send inside job-specific group chats. These are stored and may be reviewed by platform administrators for moderation purposes.

Device
FCM Token

A Firebase Cloud Messaging token unique to your device installation. Used only to deliver push notifications about jobs, chats, and platform updates.

About Device PIN / Screen Lock

During the account deletion process, the operating system may prompt you to confirm your device screen lock (PIN, Password, or Pattern). This verification is performed entirely and exclusively on your device by the operating system itself. GigsNetwork never receives, stores, transmits, or has any access to your device PIN, password, or biometric data.

We Do Not Collect

We do not collect GPS or real-time location. We do not collect browsing history, contacts, financial details, government IDs, or any data not listed above. We do not use cookies in the mobile app.

Platform Conduct, Disputes & Safety

GigsNetwork is a marketplace platform. While we provide tools to facilitate trust and safety, we are not an employer or contracting party in any gig arrangement. This section explains how we handle key edge cases.

⚠ Payment Disputes (Non-Payment by Recruiter)

GigsNetwork does not process, hold, or guarantee payments between Workers and Recruiters. All payment arrangements are agreed upon directly between the parties as stated in the job posting.

If a Recruiter fails to pay, we strongly encourage Workers to: (1) submit a report through the in-app reporting feature, (2) rate the Recruiter accordingly so other Workers are informed, and (3) seek resolution through local labour authorities or legal channels if necessary. Persistent non-payment reports may result in the Recruiter's account being reviewed or banned by our administrators.

Data we collect related to disputes: Job posting details, enrollment records, ratings, and any reports submitted will be retained and may be provided to relevant authorities in the event of a formal dispute.

🚨 Workplace Abuse, Harassment & Misconduct

GigsNetwork has zero tolerance for abuse, harassment, discrimination, or assault on the platform or at any job site facilitated through our platform. If you experience or witness such conduct:

  • If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (Police/911) first. Do not wait.
  • Use the in-app Report feature on the job, chat, or user involved to file a formal report with GigsNetwork.
  • Reports of misconduct are reviewed by our moderation team and may result in warnings, suspensions, or permanent bans.
  • We retain reports, associated messages, and account data related to misconduct and may share them with law enforcement authorities when appropriate.
🚨 Illegal Activity

Any use of GigsNetwork to facilitate, advertise, or coordinate illegal activity (including but not limited to fraud, trafficking, unlicensed work, or any criminal offence) is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate account termination.

If we become aware of illegal activity conducted on or through the platform, we are obligated to and will cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies, including disclosing user data, chat histories, and activity records as required by law. Users involved will not be notified in advance if disclosure is legally prohibited.

Our Reporting & Moderation System

Any user can report a job posting, a chat message, or another user directly within the app. Reports are stored in our database and reviewed by platform administrators who have the authority to update report status, ban users, force-close jobs, or lock chats. We aim to respond to serious safety reports promptly.

User-Generated Content

GigsNetwork allows users to create content including job postings, messages in group chats, and ratings. Please be aware of the following:

  • Group Chat Messages: Every job has a single, job-linked group chat. All messages are stored in our database and are visible to all enrolled Workers and the Recruiter for that job. Messages may also be reviewed by platform administrators for moderation purposes.
  • Ratings: Ratings and any associated feedback you submit about a Worker or Recruiter are stored permanently and displayed publicly on that user's profile.
  • Job Postings: Information entered into job postings (title, description, location, pay) is visible to all Workers on the platform searching for work in the relevant area.
  • Account Deletion & Orphaned Content: If you delete your account, your messages and enrollment records will remain as de-identified orphaned records (no longer linked to an active profile) to preserve the historical integrity of job records for other users. Ratings you received remain but your identifying information is removed.
Do Not Share Sensitive Information in Chats

Do not share bank account details, passwords, government ID numbers, or other sensitive personal information in the group chat. GigsNetwork is not liable for any harm resulting from sensitive information voluntarily shared by users in chat messages.

Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Your Right to Delete Your Account

You may permanently delete your GigsNetwork account at any time through Profile → Settings → Delete Account within the app. This right is available to all users and does not require contacting support.

When you choose to delete your account, the following happens:

1
Immediate Deactivation: Your account is locked immediately. You will be signed out and unable to log back in.
2
Profile Deletion: Your user profile including name, phone number, gender, profile picture, and location data is permanently and irreversibly erased from our active databases and Firebase Authentication records.
3
FCM Token Removal: Your device push notification token is removed, and you will no longer receive any notifications from GigsNetwork.
4
Orphaned Historical Records: Your previous job enrollments, chat messages, and attendance records will remain in our database as de-identified, orphaned records (with no link to any active profile) to maintain the data integrity and historical accuracy of records for other users who participated in those jobs or chats. These records cannot be traced back to you after deletion.
5
This action is irreversible. There is no grace period or recovery option. If you wish to use GigsNetwork again after deletion, you must create a new account.
Retention for Legal Obligations

Notwithstanding the above, we may be required to retain certain data (including records related to disputes, abuse reports, or legal proceedings) for longer periods as mandated by applicable law, even after account deletion. Such data is held in secure, restricted archives and not used for any operational purpose.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the address provided in Section 14.

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: You may correct inaccurate information (such as name or location) directly within the app's Profile settings at any time without contacting us.

We will respond to all verifiable requests within 30 days. In complex cases, we may extend this period by an additional 30 days with notice.

Third-Party Services

GigsNetwork is built on Google Firebase, a platform as a service (PaaS) provided by Google LLC. Firebase acts as a data sub-processor on our behalf. The following Firebase services are used:

  • Firebase Authentication: Handles phone number OTP sign-in. Your phone number is processed by Firebase to send and verify OTP codes.
  • Cloud Firestore: Our primary database. All user profiles, jobs, enrollments, chats, attendance records, and reports are stored here.
  • Firebase Cloud Storage: Stores uploaded profile pictures and job images.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Delivers push notifications to your device using your FCM token.

Google's processing of data through Firebase is governed by the Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review their policies at policies.google.com.

We use no other third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or data brokers in the GigsNetwork mobile application.

Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction:

  • Firebase Authentication: All sign-in is handled via Firebase's OTP system. No passwords are stored by GigsNetwork.
  • Firestore Security Rules: Database access is governed by server-side security rules that ensure users can only access data they are authorised to see.
  • HTTPS / TLS Encryption: All data transmitted between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Admin Access Controls: Our administrative panel is protected by JWT-based session authentication and accessible only to authorised personnel.
  • Data Minimisation: We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the platform.
Limitation of Security Guarantee

Despite our best efforts, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights and freedoms, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities as required by applicable law.
