Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

At GigsNetwork, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use GigsNetwork, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your phone number (used for authentication), name, profile picture, and role (Worker or Recruiter).
  • Location Data: State and City information that you manually select to help us show you relevant, nearby jobs.
  • Activity Data: Jobs posted, jobs enrolled in, ratings given/received, and messages sent within job-specific group chats.
  • Device Information: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to send you push notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to provide and improve our services, specifically to:

  • Authenticate your account using OTP verification.
  • Match Workers with relevant jobs in their selected State and City.
  • Facilitate communication between Workers and Recruiters via Group Chats.
  • Maintain trust and safety on the platform through our rating system.

About Device PIN/Passwords: During the account deletion process, we may ask you to enter your device's screen lock PIN/Password/Pattern. This is evaluated strictly locally on your device by the operating system. GigsNetwork never stores, transmits, or has access to your device PIN.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Information is only shared within the platform to facilitate gig work:

  • When a Worker enrolls in a job, their name, profile picture, and phone number are shared with the Recruiter.
  • Recruiter details (Organization name, phone number, ratings) are visible to Workers viewing their job postings.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information as long as your account is active. You have the right to delete your account at any time through the app settings.

When you delete your account:

  • Your primary user profile and authentication records are permanently erased from our active databases.
  • Your previous enrollments and chat messages will remain as orphaned records to maintain the integrity of historical data for other users, but they will no longer be linked to an active profile.
  • This action is irreversible.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including Firebase Authentication, to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy practices, please contact us at support@gigsnetwork.com.
