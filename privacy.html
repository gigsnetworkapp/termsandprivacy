Section 01

Who We Are & Scope of This Policy

GigsNetwork is a two-sided gig marketplace mobile application operated by Mugesh Siva, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. The app connects Workers seeking short-term event and hospitality work with Recruiters who post such opportunities.

This Privacy Policy describes every category of personal data we collect when you use the GigsNetwork Android application, the purposes for which we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it.

ℹ️

Platform Intermediary Notice

GigsNetwork acts strictly as a marketplace intermediary. We are not an employer, staffing agency, or payment processor. We facilitate connections between Workers and Recruiters but are not a party to any employment or payment agreement.

Section 05

Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only in these specific, limited circumstances:

  • Other Users (Platform Function): When you enroll in a job, your name, profile picture, and phone number are shared with the Recruiter. Recruiter's name, organisation, phone number, and ratings are visible to Workers viewing their listings. This is essential to the platform's purpose.
  • Platform Administrators: Our admin team can access user profiles, job data, and chat histories strictly for moderation, investigating reports, and banning bad actors. Access is restricted and logged.
  • Firebase / Google (Data Processor): GigsNetwork is built on Google Firebase. Firebase processes data on our behalf for authentication, database, storage, and notifications. See Section 10 for details.
  • Law Enforcement / Legal Process: We will disclose user data to government authorities when legally required by a valid court order or applicable law. We will inform affected users where we are legally permitted to do so.
  • Protection of Rights: We may disclose data in good faith when necessary to prevent imminent harm, investigate fraud, or protect the rights of GigsNetwork, our users, or the public.
⚠️

No Sale of Personal Data

GigsNetwork has never sold and will never sell your personal information to advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for commercial purposes.

Section 06

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

  • Active Account Data (name, phone, gender, location, profile photo, FCM token): Retained for the lifetime of your account and permanently deleted upon account deletion.
  • Chat Messages: Stored while the associated job is active and for a period thereafter. Chats become read-only 3 days after the job's end date. Upon account deletion, your messages are de-identified (stripped of your name and profile link).
  • Job Enrollments & Attendance Records: Retained as de-identified, anonymised records after account deletion to preserve historical integrity for the Recruiter's records.
  • Ratings: Ratings you received remain on record after deletion but your identifying information is removed.
  • Abuse Reports & Legal Data: Data related to active moderation actions, disputes, or legal proceedings may be retained beyond deletion for a period required by applicable Indian law.

Section 07

Account Deletion — Your Right to Erase

✅

You Can Delete Your Account At Any Time

You may permanently delete your GigsNetwork account at any time, free of charge, directly from within the app. No contact with support is needed.

How to Delete Your Account (In-App)

1

Open the GigsNetwork app and go to the Profile tab.

2

Tap Settings.

3

Tap "Delete Account".

4

Your device's operating system may prompt you to verify your screen lock (PIN, fingerprint, or face). This is handled entirely by your device — GigsNetwork never receives, stores, or transmits your PIN or biometric data.

5

Confirm the deletion. Your account is immediately and permanently deleted.

Alternatively — Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app, you may also request account deletion by emailing us at hmsolutions.tech.pvt@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and the phone number linked to your account. We will process your request within 30 days.

What Is Permanently Deleted

  • Your phone number and Firebase Authentication record
  • Your full name, gender, and location data
  • Your profile picture (deleted from Firebase Cloud Storage)
  • Your device's FCM push notification token

What Remains (Anonymised Only)

  • Past job enrollment records — retained as de-identified orphaned records (linked to a job ID only, not to your identity) to preserve historical data for Recruiters.
  • Chat messages — may remain as text-only orphaned records within job chat history, with your name and profile link permanently removed.
  • Attendance records — anonymised for Recruiter reporting integrity.
⛔

Deletion is Permanent & Irreversible

There is no grace period or recovery option. Once deleted, you cannot recover your account. To use GigsNetwork again, you must register a new account. A banned user cannot re-register with the same phone number.

Section 10

Third-Party Services

GigsNetwork is built on Google Firebase (provided by Google LLC), which acts as a data sub-processor on our behalf. We use the following Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication: Handles phone number OTP sign-in. Your phone number is processed by Firebase to send and verify OTP codes.
  • Cloud Firestore: Our primary database. Stores all user profiles, jobs, enrollments, chat messages, attendance records, and reports.
  • Firebase Cloud Storage: Stores profile pictures and any images attached to job postings.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Delivers push notifications to your device using your FCM token.

Google's processing is governed by the Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy, available at policies.google.com.

No Other Third Parties

We use no other third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or data brokers in the GigsNetwork mobile application. Firebase is our only external service provider.

Section 11

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • OTP-Only Authentication: No passwords are stored by GigsNetwork. All sign-in is handled via Firebase's secure OTP system.
  • Firestore Security Rules: Server-side rules enforce that users can only access data they are authorised to see (e.g., only enrolled Workers can read a job's chat).
  • Server-Side Validation: All critical writes (posting jobs, enrolling, rating) go through Firebase Cloud Functions — not directly from the client — preventing data manipulation.
  • TLS / HTTPS Encryption: All data transmitted between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit.
  • Data Minimisation: We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the platform.
⚠️

No Absolute Guarantee

No method of internet transmission is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that materially affects your rights, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities as required by applicable law.
