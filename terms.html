🚫

Non-Payment is a Serious Violation

Failure or refusal to pay Workers as agreed in the job posting is a serious breach of these Terms. Recruiters who do not pay will face investigation, suspension, and permanent account banning. Repeat offenders may be reported to relevant authorities.

GigsNetwork is not a party to any payment agreement and cannot compel payment or provide financial compensation from its own funds. We can assist by providing records, investigating reports, and banning non-paying Recruiters, but we do not guarantee payment outcomes.

Job postings created with the intent to lure Workers without genuine intent to hire or pay constitute fraud. Such accounts will be immediately and permanently banned and may be reported to law enforcement authorities.

7.5 Worker Verification

Workers are strongly advised to confirm payment terms in writing via the job's group chat before commencing work. Chat records are preserved within the platform and can serve as evidence in a dispute.

Section 12

Attendance System

For multi-day or multi-session jobs, GigsNetwork provides an attendance tracking system. Recruiters may mark Workers as Present or Absent for each session. Attendance data is used to:

  • Generate reports for the Recruiter's internal record-keeping
  • Facilitate payment calculations for hourly or per-session pay structures
  • Provide accountability and transparency for both parties

Workers should note that their attendance record is visible to the Recruiter and may be referenced in case of a payment dispute. GigsNetwork does not alter attendance records; any disputes about a record must be raised with the Recruiter directly via the group chat.

Section 18

Your Right to Delete Your Account

✓

You Control Your Data

You have the right to permanently delete your GigsNetwork account at any time, free of charge, directly from within the App. No contact with support is required.

18.1 How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the GigsNetwork App
  2. Navigate to Profile tab
  3. Tap Settings
  4. Tap "Delete Account"
  5. Complete the identity verification step (your device may prompt for your screen lock PIN or biometric — this is handled entirely by your device's operating system and is never transmitted to GigsNetwork)
  6. Confirm the deletion when prompted

18.2 What Is Permanently Deleted

  • Your phone number and all personal identifiers
  • Your name and profile picture (removed from Firebase Storage)
  • Your gender and location information
  • Your Firebase Authentication record (you cannot log in again with the same phone number unless you re-register)
  • Your device's push notification (FCM) token

18.3 What May Remain (Anonymised)

  • Past enrollment records are anonymised (linked to a job ID, not your identity) to maintain historical integrity for Recruiters' records
  • Past chat messages may remain as orphaned records within job chat history — they will not be linked to your profile or name
  • Attendance records are anonymised for reporting integrity

18.4 Important Notes

⚠

Deletion is Irreversible

Account deletion is permanent. You cannot undo it. Any active job enrollments at the time of deletion will be automatically cancelled. Recruiters with active job listings should close them before deleting. Deletion does not cancel any payment obligations owed to Workers.
